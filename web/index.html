Tell the robot what to do

The AI writes a program for your words (a few cents). Before you decide to run it, it is checked, compiled to machine code and rehearsed on a copy of the table.

More options

The robot cell live

Drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom. A simulated arm with real joint limits and speeds: every move you see is planned and carried out by the simulator, not animated for show. The blue see-through arm replays a rehearsal.

Nothing running

Choose what should happen above and press Start.

The robot pauses (the move in progress finishes first). The AI writes a new program for the table as it is then; you see it checked and rehearsed before it takes over. Moves already made stay.

Pause and Stop control the software only; they are not an emergency stop for real machinery.
